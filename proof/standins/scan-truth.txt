Notice to Members: Annual Meeting
The annual meeting of the Valley Garden Club will be held at the community hall on
Saturday, May 16, starting at ten in the morning. All members are welcome, and guests
may attend the open session after lunch.

The agenda includes the election of two new board members, a short report on the
seed library, and a vote on the proposed change to the yearly dues. Copies of the
budget will be available at the door.

Volunteers are needed to set up chairs and tables on Friday evening. If you can help,
please call the club office or leave a note in the box by the front gate. Light
refreshments will be served after the meeting.

We thank everyone who donated plants and tools to the spring sale. The money raised
will pay for new raised beds at the school garden and a rain barrel for the shared plot on
Elm Street.
