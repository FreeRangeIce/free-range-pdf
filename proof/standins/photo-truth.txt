Rental Agreement Summary
This summary covers the lease of the second floor apartment at 42 Oak Avenue. The
lease begins on the first day of June and runs for twelve months, ending on the last day of
May next year.

The monthly rent is due on the first of each month and may be paid by check or bank
transfer. A late fee will apply if payment is more than five days late. The security deposit
equals one month of rent.

The tenant is responsible for electricity and internet service. The landlord pays for water,
trash pickup, and yard care. Pets are allowed with written approval and a small extra
deposit.

Either party may end the lease early with sixty days of written notice. Please keep this
summary with your signed copy of the full agreement for future reference.
