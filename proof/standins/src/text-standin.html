Free Range Farm Cooperative: Spring Notes

The cooperative met on the first Monday of April to review the planting schedule for the coming season. Members agreed that the north field will be sown with oats and barley, while the south field will rest under clover until late summer.

Our egg sales rose by eleven percent compared with last year. Most of the growth came from the Saturday market in town, where the stall now opens an hour earlier. The hens are healthy, and the new coop doors have held up well through the wet weather.

The water pump by the old barn still needs repair. A volunteer crew will meet at the barn on Thursday morning with tools and spare parts. Please bring gloves and a flask of tea, because the work may take most of the day.

Finally, the board thanks every family who helped with the winter feed drive. Your time and effort kept the flock well fed during the coldest weeks of the year, and we look forward to seeing you at the summer picnic.
